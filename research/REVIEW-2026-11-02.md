# November 2 accuracy review

November 1 Discord final card: 4 wins / 6 losses, ten final recommendations. No bets on CLE–PIT or IND–JAX; superseded lines are not extra bets.

point_candidates.py compares five predefined points-model variants with chronological weekly ratings and ridge fits. Selection uses 2025 only; the existing 2026 weeks 5–7 period is reported without selecting parameters on it. The current model wins screening Brier on 272 selection games. Normal probabilities are only a quick screening approximation, not the deployed drive simulation. November 1 outcomes never select parameters. Historical periods have been used in prior model work, so they are not newly untouched data.

joint_calibration.py studies a per-match correction to the simulator's target means. It roughly halves mean-target numerical error, with tiny proper-score improvement in 2025, but worsens Brier and distribution CRPS in the 43-game 2026 comparison. It is not promoted. See the two JSON reports. Core weights and simulation coefficients stay unchanged.

Shipped: probability and fee-inclusive EV must clear 1.96 Monte Carlo standard errors at the final selector, including custom selectors. Half-credit ties contribute to expected settlement value but cannot push an outright win probability below 50% into a recommendation. Both preferred and fallback adapters preserve outright win probability. Numerical sampling error does not quantify model uncertainty or establish profitable future bets.
