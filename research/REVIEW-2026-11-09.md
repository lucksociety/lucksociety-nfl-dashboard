## Recommendation screening

`probability-stress.py` replays the existing chronological point-model reconstruction for 272 games in 2025. It prices fixed diagnostic half-point spreads -21.5 to +21.5 and totals 19.5 to 65.5, plus game winners, without any market prices. Bootstrap whole weeks (3,000 replicates) for the average overconfidence of model-favoured sides. Rounded upper 97.5th-percentile reserves: moneyline .050, spread .018, total .019. These are screening stress allowances, not individual prediction confidence intervals or a historical trading backtest. Fixed lines are correlated; historical periods have been used in earlier research. Mean overconfidence is near zero; the positive reserve reflects uncertainty, not proof of systematic bias. The 43 games in 2026 weeks 5–7 are reported separately, not used to select reserves and not described as a newly untouched holdout.

Inputs are preserved in `probability-stress-inputs.csv`, hash-identified in the JSON report. Reproduce with `python research/probability-stress.py --data research/probability-stress-inputs.csv --out /tmp/nfl-probability-stress.json`. This is a historical reconstruction, not an archive of live injury knowledge. Subtract the reserve and Monte Carlo error for both outright win probability and settlement value; both must still clear 50% and fee-inclusive cost respectively. This intentionally reduces borderline recommendations, including some eventual winners. No guarantee of improved future hit rate or return follows from this stress rule.

Among qualifying bets, rank conservative outright win probability first and ROI second, consistent with the requested win-rate priority. Keep positive EV, actual displayed-score agreement, liquidity, availability and pregame guards.

## Concrete integrity fixes

- An unavailable live Jupiter feed no longer silently substitutes a repository snapshot. Rerun when live executable quotes can be fetched. New commands clear both preferred and fallback event caches.
- Unknown teams and ambiguous total sides are not interpreted as the away team or Under.
- Spread probabilities already use each side's own handicap parsed from its label. The exported candidate used the raw provider `sportsLine`, which can instead describe the favourite. Export that same parsed own handicap so score checks and later settlement refer to the exact contract whose probability was computed. A KC +3.5 / BUF -3.5 fixture reproduces this mismatch. This is a potential error found in review, not an established cause of November 8 losses.

## Learn from the bets actually delivered

The daily learner previously tracked game winners but did not preserve the actual NFL spread/total recommendations. The workflow now saves structured primary bets alongside frozen model output and writes a credential-free receipt only after every Discord message succeeds. Capture requires that receipt, pregame publication and recent quotes. Store full win probability, fee-inclusive cost, contract side/own handicap and a distinct selection-policy version. Alternatives are excluded; the latest pregame NO BET supersedes an older bet. Failed/partial delivery creates no receipt. No invented historical Discord records are imported.

Daily learning collects these immutable hashed records, grades final scores at the actual handicap/total, and reports win rate, probability calibration and hypothetical price-based ROI separately by model, policy and market. Proposed calibration still requires the existing sample-size and chronological validation gates; no automatic weight promotion. Actual fills/stakes remain unknown.

Validation: model JS/Python parity, probability stress vs its research evidence, preferred/fallback/custom selectors, displayed-score/pregame guards, unknown contracts, own-handicap mapping, fresh-price failures, cache reset, successful/partial/failed delivery receipts, archived record integrity, NO BET supersession and settlement regressions.
