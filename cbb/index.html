How it works

Several times a day a GitHub Action refreshes the season's results, refits the team ratings and simulates every listed men's game 300,000 times. The ratings estimate each team's offence and defence per 100 possessions, adjusted for opponents, plus its tempo and home advantage; neutral-site games get no home advantage. The spread of simulated scores comes from how far the model's own past forecasts missed at the same stage of earlier seasons.

The Simulator tab draws 100,000 games from those stored results for one matchup. It cannot change who plays: this model rates teams, not players. Instead it shows what the model could confirm about availability, which decides whether a bet may be posted in Discord.

What it covers. Only men's games that Polymarket lists, because the Discord command prices those. Polymarket is read here for the list of games only. Once a game tips off, the page keeps the last forecast made before the tip.

Honesty notes. Early in the season most teams have played only a few games, so forecasts lean on last season and are less certain. Exact scores and the displayed point totals are approximations. Bets are posted only in Discord by /cbb; nothing on this page is betting advice.
