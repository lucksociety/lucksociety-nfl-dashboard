# College basketball v1.1 — matchup-conditioned score uncertainty

November 19's delivered card went 4/10 on bets and 20/30 on winner forecasts. Santa Clara +2.5 (85.2%), UMBC +31.5 (76.1%) and Pepperdine +3.5 (73.9%) lost. These are retained as losses; no historical recommendation is rewritten.

The v1.0 simulator resampled one global paired score-error pool per calendar phase, regardless of whether a matchup was balanced or had a large projected favorite. V1.1 weights past errors by proximity of their predicted home margin to the current predicted margin. A 20-point Gaussian bandwidth and 75% local / 25% global mixture were selected on chronological 2025 evaluation only, from four declared protocols. Both team errors stay paired, preserving correlation and empirical tails. The global mixture retains support for unusual results. All residuals and their forecast contexts predate the prediction cutoff by at least four hours; no price enters the model.

The existing ridge/retention rating parameters are retained. The new protocol is used on every fresh `/cbb` forecast, and records its error-model identity and effective residual count. All >50%, score-alignment, fees, uncertainty-reserve, fresh-book and execution-depth requirements remain.

The improvement is modest, not a leap to a reliable 80% betting win rate. Current-season total MAE is slightly worse. The exact distribution screen improves winner log loss and margin CRPS in all three periods; the current-season total-CRPS difference is indistinguishable from zero. This is score/probability validation, not a historical execution or ROI backtest. Phases and teams are pooled; game-level standard errors in the screen do not account for all date/team correlation. Historical box scores and schedule metadata are revised source archives, not immutable snapshots of every original feed.

## Reproduction and safeguards

`python -m cbb.research_errors` builds strictly chronological rating residuals from SportsDataverse's ESPN archive, cached locally as `data/research-residuals.json` (not committed). The simulator confirmation uses those same rows with the baseline uniform and new weighted sampler. `python -m pytest -q cbb` verifies cutoff alignment, paired-error preservation, reproducibility, broad support, corner symmetry, roster/price checks and existing flows. The CBB CI now runs all CBB tests; daily evaluation also refreshes when the model changes.
